Welcome to Minecraft Bedrock Edition speedrunning! Comprehensive guides to help you get started and improve your runs.

Speedrunning means completing a game as fast as possible. In Minecraft: Bedrock Edition, you beat the game by killing the Ender Dragon. You can do it on a random seed (RSG), a set seed (SSG) or a filtered seed (FSG). The best version for RSG and FSG is 1.16.1.

Get Started
Welcome to Bedrock Ranked!

Ranked is a concept where you compete with fellow runners to see who can complete a seed fastest.

There are currently 8 modes in Ranked:
stronghold village 1.16, ruined portal 1.16, village 1.16, desert temple 1.16, shipwreck 1.16, warped forest 1.18+, ruined portal 1.18+ and bastion 1.18+.

To start, you will need to join the discord server, and get the correct version of Minecraft Bedrock Edition (either 1.16 or any version 1.21.60+). If you are a console player you'll have to get the @packless role from the channel ranks-and-roles, otherwise you won't be able to play. You'll also need a recording software to record your ranked runs.
Rules
To start playing:

• Go to the queue channel and click on the queue you want to join based on the version you want to play in.
• When an opponent is found, you will be pinged and the match will start in a dedicated thread.
• In the matches, don't spam ping your opponent, answer to pings, never use commands or creative or any other form of cheating (where not stated, speedrun.com rules apply) and report issues in the dedicated channel.

Violations may result in penalties or bans.
FAQ
Q: How does this work?
A: Bedrock Ranked is hosted through a discord bot in the server. On this website you can get statistics.

Q: What is an add-on?
A: An add-on is a collection of behavior and resource packs, which modify some aspect of the game. Download the .mcaddon file from packs and open it with Minecraft.

Q: Is this viable on console?
A: Yes! Just make sure to choose "1.18+" and "no pack" when prompted. You will play warped forest 1.18+ seeds as they don't require any pack.
